Here is the definitive instruction set for performing a standard, foundational layout at home: Step 1: Formulating the Query – A successful reading depends entirely on clarity. The querent must focus on an open-ended, active question rather than a passive binary prompt: Do not ask: "Will I get the job next Monday?" Ask: "What energies should I focus on to improve my professional career trajectory?" Step 2: The Shuffling Sequence • Clear the workspace of clutter to enhance visual contrast against your card stock. Shuffle the 78-card deck using whatever physical shuffling style is preferred (overhand, riffle, or washing). • While shuffling, mentally hold the formulated query in focus to conceptually "program" the random array. • Place the deck face down and have the person receiving the reading cut the cards into three piles using their left hand, then restack them in any order. Step 3: Laying Out the Standard Three-Card Spread The most universal, dynamic, and scannable layout engine used today is the Three-Card Spread. Deal three consecutive cards face down from left to right, then flip them over to analyze the following positions: Card 1 (Left Position): The Past / Root Causes Function: Details the foundational energies, past decisions, or ancestral habits that built the current situation. Card 2 (Center Position): The Present / Immediate Obstacles Function: Illustrates the immediate environment, active emotional frameworks, and hidden conflicts occurring right now.
